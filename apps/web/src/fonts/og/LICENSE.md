# Fonts for share cards (Open Graph images)

`sora-800.ttf` (Sora ExtraBold), `jetbrains-mono-600.ttf` (JetBrains Mono SemiBold) and
`noto-serif-jp-700-subset.woff` (Noto Serif JP Bold, subset to 上下的中外れ無効) are from Google Fonts, all under the SIL
Open Font License 1.1 (full text: `OFL.txt`). TTF and WOFF because `next/og` (Satori) reads TTF, OTF and WOFF but not
WOFF2. Fetched 2026-11-18 from fonts.gstatic.com.
