| | Sensei | Agents |
|---|---|---|
| **What it is** | A coach in a side panel | A player you build |
| **Who signs** | You, always: its trade cards open your normal entry | Ask you: you sign its proposal. Plays alone: the agent's own key, from its vault |
| **Money it can touch** | None | Its vault, only as entries that pay you, each under its cap |
| **Model** | `openai/gpt-5.4-mini` by default (`AI_MODEL`) | The same, or no model at all for **Partner** |
| **Brakes** | Tells you to skip a round; points to your play limits | Confidence floor, crowding limit, loss breaker, daily cap |
