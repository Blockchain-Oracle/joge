## Sensei

Tap the orb (on a phone, the seal in the dock) and ask for a read. Sensei gives a straight call, <Up />, <Down /> or sit out, with one reason and one risk, and up to two ticket cards you can enter with one tap. It never signs anything. It hears this browser's recent results and pace, never your address, and after a losing run or four asks in three minutes it tells you to slow down and points to your limits.

| | Sensei | Agents |
|---|---|---|
| Who signs | you, always | you (Asks you) or its own key, from its vault (Plays alone) |
| Money it touches | none | its vault, only as bets that pay you |
| Model | `openai/gpt-5.4-mini` by default | the same, or none for Partners |
