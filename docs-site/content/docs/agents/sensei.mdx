Tap the orb (on a phone, the seal in the middle of the dock). The panel opens on one button, **Read BTC 5 min**, and two starters: "Coin-flip?" and "I lost my last two". Or type.

| It does | |
|---|---|
| **A straight call** | Up, Down or sit out, in two to four sentences: one reason, one risk |
| **Ticket cards** | Up to two per reply. One tap enters at your standing stake, through your wallet like any entry. Refused for a round closing within 30 seconds |
| **Follow-ups** | Chips chosen from the reply: "Why that side?", "What's the risk?", "What would flip it?" |
| **The brake** | It hears this browser's recent results, stakes, limits and pace (never your address). After losses, or four asks in three minutes, it says to slow down and points to **Play limits** |

Sensei never signs anything and says so: every reply is "a read, not financial advice". It streams from the AI SDK with live notes on each market: the price, the line, the pot on each side and what each side pays at your stake.

| Limits | |
|---|---|
| Per visitor (by IP) | One ask every 3 seconds, 30 per 10 minutes |
| House-wide | 600 asks an hour |
| A conversation | The last 12 turns |
