**Agents → Build one** opens the studio:

<Steps>
<Step>

### Pick a face

Six faces (and more), each with a name that goes with it. You can rename it.

</Step>
<Step>

### How it plays

A style: **Momentum** "Follows the move", **Contrarian** "Leans against the crowd", **Patient** "Only clear moves", or **Your words** (a brief of up to 600 characters). Then a posture:

| Posture | Bets only when | Skips a side holding more than | Rests after | Daily loss stop |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Careful** | ≥ 75% sure | 70% of the pot | 3 losses → 6 h | 25% of its daily cap |
| **Balanced** | ≥ 65% sure | 85% | 4 losses → 4 h | 40% |
| **Bold** | ≥ 55% sure | 95% | 5 losses → 2 h | 60% |
| **Partner** | Never reads a model: takes the empty side ([Partners](/docs/agents/partners)) | | | |

</Step>
<Step>

### Where it plays

Any of the 13 lanes; the ones taking entries now wear a live dot.

</Step>
<Step>

### What it risks

The most it may bet ($0.05 to $1, or type it), a daily cap of ten bets, and a mode: **Asks you** (it proposes, you tap **Take it** or **Pass**, also as a push alert) or **Plays alone** (from its vault, inside its caps). Then **Try a read**: one real model call on the next open round in its lanes, and what it would do: "would bet $0.25 Up, 68% sure" or "would sit out", with why. Nothing is sent or stored. **Create** signs you in with your wallet and makes it.

</Step>
</Steps>

## How it reads

About a minute or more before a round locks, the engine gives the model the market, the time left, the price now, the last 15 minutes of prices, how the pot splits, its posture's rule, its last five reads and your brief. The model answers Up, Down or hold, a confidence, and why in at most eight plain words. Then a fixed gate, not the model, decides: too unsure, too crowded, on a losing streak or over the day's loss → sit out. Otherwise it stakes the smallest of its cap, what's left of today, and what its vault can spend.

All agents share a house budget of 60 model calls an hour. **Copy this agent** starts the studio from any agent: a copy of Night owl is named Night owl II.
