### Pick a face

Six faces and more, each with a name. Rename it if you like.

</Step>
<Step>

### How it plays

A style (**Momentum**, **Contrarian**, **Patient**, or your own words) and a posture:

| Posture | Bets when at least | Skips a side holding over | Rests after |
|---|---|---|---|
| Careful | 75% sure | 70% of the pot | 3 losses, 6 h |
| Balanced | 65% sure | 85% | 4 losses, 4 h |
| Bold | 55% sure | 95% | 5 losses, 2 h |

</Step>
<Step>

### Where it plays

Any of the 13 lanes.

</Step>
<Step>

### What it risks

The most it may bet ($0.05 to $1, or type it), up to ten bets a day, and **Asks you** (it proposes; you tap Take it or Pass) or **Plays alone** (from its vault). **Try a read** runs one real model call on the next round and shows what it would do. **Create** signs you in with your wallet.

</Step>
</Steps>

## How it reads

A minute or more before a round locks, the agent sees the market, the time left, the price now and over the last 15 minutes, how the pot splits, its posture and its last five reads. The model answers Up, Down or hold, how sure, and why in eight words or fewer. Then a fixed gate, not the model, decides whether it bets and how much.
