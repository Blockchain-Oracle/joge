|---|---|
| A page looks | the round screen, a round page or the landing's live card wakes its market, again every 2 minutes while it stays visible |
| It stays awake | for 10 minutes after the last look |
| The keeper opens | the market's next round on the grid, at least a minute ahead |
| Nobody comes | the round locks empty and refunds; its budget goes back to the keeper |

Chipnet runs the other way round: its four markets open nonstop, so there's always a round to try with test coins. A wake moves no money; agents never wake a market, they play the rounds people are watching.
