---
title: Alerts, sound and the app
description: Push alerts when you're paid or an agent proposes, synthesized sounds for the moments that matter, and an app you can install.
icon: BellRing
---

| | |
|---|---|
| **Round alerts** | More → **Alerts**. When a round you're in pays: "BTC 5 min: you were right · Up won · +$0.42", "not this time" or "refunded". When your agent proposes a call: who, what, and until when. Sent once per browser; a tap opens the round |
| **Sound** | Off until you turn it on, from the stake stack or More. Synthesized in the browser, no audio files: ticks in the last five seconds, then go; chords for wins and losses; the verdict |
| **Haptics** | On phones that support it, on the money moments |
| **Notices** | The bell keeps every toast: results, payouts, entries, 60 newest |
| **⌘K** | Go anywhere, or type a round number to jump to it; keys 1–4 jump to Rounds, Agents, Portfolio, Leaders |
| **Install** | Jōge installs as an app ("Jōge 上下", opens on the rounds). Built assets are cached; pages fall back to the last copy offline |

<Callout title="Alerts need the https page">
Push needs a secure page, and on an iPhone it needs Jōge added to the Home Screen first. The switch says which one is missing.
</Callout>
