<Steps>
<Step>

### What it is

One screen, one sentence. **Skip** is always there.

</Step>
<Step>

### Get a wallet

Any Bitcoin Cash wallet that speaks WizardConnect: <Logo name="cashonize.png" /> Cashonize, <Logo name="paytaca.png" /> Paytaca, <Logo name="selene.svg" /> Selene.

</Step>
<Step>

### Pair it

Scan the code, tap **Open wallet** on a phone, or **Open in Cashonize** on a desktop. Your wallet sees Jōge's name and icon. You pair once; a refresh keeps it.

</Step>
<Step>

### Get coins

On chipnet, **Get test BCH** tops your wallet up to 2,000,000 test sats, about twenty calls. On mainnet, the guide shows your address and its QR code.

</Step>
<Step>

### Call it

Tap <Up /> or <Down />. Your wallet signs one transaction: that's the entry.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Chipnet or mainnet

<Crop src="/shots/crop/switch.webp" alt="Switch to mainnet: Real BCH from here, and Slide to use real money" caption="Going to mainnet takes a slide; coming back takes one tap." width={420} />

| | 試 Chipnet | 本 Mainnet |
|---|---|---|
| Money | Free test coins | Real BCH |
| Coins | **Get test BCH**, in the app | Send BCH to your wallet |
| Rounds | Nonstop on all four markets | While someone is watching ([5.2](/docs/mainnet/on-demand)) |

The faucet tops up a wallet holding under 500,000 test sats, once per address every 12 hours and five times per device a day. The guide reopens from **More** or ⌘K.
