The first time you open the rounds, the screen dims and a ring circles one thing at a time, with one line beside it. Tap **Next**, or use the arrow keys. **Skip** is always there.

<Steps>
<Step>

### The question

**Will Bitcoin go up or down?** Guess whether the price ends higher or lower. Guess right and you split the pot.

</Step>
<Step>

### The clock

Bets close when the ring runs out. The price at that moment is [the line to beat](/docs/price/the-line). When the round ends, the price is above it (<Up /> wins) or below it (<Down /> wins).

</Step>
<Step>

### Your bet

Tap an amount, or **Other** for your own. It stays picked for every bet after.

</Step>
<Step>

### Up or Down

One tap and your wallet signs. The number on each button is what a win pays per dollar: 1.8× means a $1 bet pays back $1.80.

</Step>
<Step>

### Test coins

The switch at the top says which money you're playing with. A first visit starts on free test coins.

</Step>
<Step>

### How to play

Replays the walkthrough. It's on the round screen, in **More** and in ⌘K.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Then pair a wallet

<Crop src="/shots/crop/pair.webp" alt="Pair a wallet: a WizardConnect code, wallet logos, Open wallet and Copy link" caption="Pair once; a refresh keeps it." width={360} />

The walkthrough ends on **Pair a wallet**:

1. **Get a wallet.** Any Bitcoin Cash wallet that speaks WizardConnect: <Logo name="cashonize.png" /> Cashonize (in your browser), <Logo name="paytaca.png" /> Paytaca, <Logo name="selene.svg" /> Selene.
2. **Pair it.** Scan the code, or tap **Open wallet** on a phone. Your wallet shows Jōge's name and icon.
3. **Get coins.** On test coins, **Get test BCH** fills your wallet in one tap. On mainnet, Jōge reads what your wallet already holds and never asks you to send it anything. Your BCH stays in your wallet until you sign a bet.

|---|---|---|
| Money | Free test coins | Real BCH |
| Where you start | Every first visit | One switch away |
| Coins | **Get test BCH**, in the app | Already in your wallet |
| Rounds | Nonstop on all four markets | While someone is watching ([5.2](/docs/mainnet/on-demand)) |

<Crop src="/shots/crop/switch.webp" alt="Switch to mainnet: Real BCH from here, and Slide to use real money" caption="Going to mainnet takes a slide; coming back takes one tap. Your choice is kept on this device." width={420} />

The faucet tops up a wallet holding under 500,000 test sats to 2,000,000 (about twenty bets), once per address every 12 hours and five times a day per visitor (counted by IP address).
