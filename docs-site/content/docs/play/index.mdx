Every few minutes a new round opens on each market. You tap **Up** or **Down**; your wallet signs one entry; your stake joins the round's pot. When the round closes, a contract on Bitcoin Cash reads the price General Protocols signed, and the side that called it splits the whole pot. The payout lands in your wallet by itself.

There is no house taking the other side, no account, and no server that can keep your money: the round's coins sit in covenants that only pay out by the rules.

## The four markets

| Market | Round | Price from |
|---|---|---|
| <Logo name="coin-btc.svg" /> Bitcoin | 5 minutes | General Protocols USD/BTC |
| <Logo name="coin-eth.svg" /> Ether | 5 minutes | General Protocols USD/ETH |
| <Logo name="coin-bch.svg" /> Bitcoin Cash | 5 minutes | General Protocols USD/BCH |
| <Logo name="coin-gold.svg" /> Gold | 1 hour | USD/BCH and XAU/BCH, combined on chain |

The chain carries 13 lanes in all (3, 5 and 15 minutes on BTC, ETH and BCH; one hour on all four). The app plays the four above; agents can play any of the 13.

## What a round looks like

| | |
|---|---|
| **Stake** | From 5,000 sats (about a cent) to 500,000 sats on 5-minute rounds, 5,000,000 on gold. The app's ladder runs $0.05 to $10, or type any amount |
| **Pays if right** | Your share of the whole pot: your stake × pot ÷ your side's total. The plates show it live as **1.17×** |
| **Fees** | 2% of the pot to the protocol, 0.5% to whoever opened the round; a fee under 1,000 sats is not taken and stays with the winners |
| **Refunds** | Nobody on the other side, a tie, or a frozen price: everyone gets their stake back |
| **Who pays out** | The contract. Anyone can push the payout transaction: our keeper, your browser, a stranger's |
