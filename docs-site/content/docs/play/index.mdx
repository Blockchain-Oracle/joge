---
title: What Jōge is
description: "Call BTC, ETH, BCH or gold up or down for a few cents. A contract on Bitcoin Cash checks the signed price and pays the winners; nobody runs the house."
---

A round opens every 5 minutes on Bitcoin, Ether and Bitcoin Cash and every hour on gold (on mainnet, while someone is watching). You call <Up /> or <Down /> against <Line />, your wallet signs one entry, and your stake joins the round's pot. At the close a contract reads the price General Protocols signed, and the side that called it splits the pot, less the fees on bigger pots ([2.2](/docs/round/payouts)). The payout lands in your wallet by itself.

<Facts>
  <Fact label="Markets">Bitcoin, Ether and Bitcoin Cash every 5 minutes; gold every hour.</Fact>
  <Fact label="Stakes">From about a cent. The app's ladder runs $0.05 to $10 a tap, cut at each market's cap (about $1 on the 5-minute markets), or type any amount within those bounds.</Fact>
  <Fact label="No house">A shared pot. If only one side plays, or the close ties the line, everyone is refunded.</Fact>
  <Fact label="Who pays">The contract, straight to the address on your ticket. Anyone can send the payout.</Fact>
</Facts>

## Your money never passes through Jōge

The entry you sign moves your stake from your wallet straight into the round's contract, and the ticket it makes carries your payout address. Your browser reads coins and broadcasts through public Bitcoin Cash servers, not through our database. [How a round works](/docs/round).
