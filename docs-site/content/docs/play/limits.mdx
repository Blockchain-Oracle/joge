| Limit | Choices | The rule |
|---|---|---|
| Daily limit | None · $1 · $5 · $10 · any amount | Lowering applies now; raising waits a day |
| Break | 1 hour · 24 hours · 7 days | Can't be ended early |

The Up and Down buttons wear a limit first: "Over your daily limit · $0.40 left today", "Cooling off until 21:30". Sensei knows your limits too, and points you here after a losing run.

## Alerts and the app

| | |
|---|---|
| Alerts | When a round you're in pays, or your agent proposes a call. More → Alerts |
| Sound | Off until you turn it on: ticks in the last seconds, chords for wins and losses |
| Notices | The bell keeps every result and payout |
| ⌘K | Go anywhere, or type a round number |
| Install | Add Jōge to your home screen; it opens on the rounds |

> Alerts need the https page; on an iPhone, add Jōge to the Home Screen first.
