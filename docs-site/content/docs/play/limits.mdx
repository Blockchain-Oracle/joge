---
title: Play limits
description: A daily limit and a break you set yourself, built so you can't undo them in the heat of a losing streak.
icon: ShieldHalf
---

In **More**, under the wallet:

| Limit | Choices | The rule |
|---|---|---|
| **Daily limit** | No limit · $1 · $5 · $10 · Other | Lowering applies now. Raising or removing waits a day |
| **Break** | 1 hour · 24 hours · 7 days | Can't be ended early |

The Up and Down plates wear a limit before anything else: "Over your daily limit · $0.40 left today", "Daily limit reached · back tomorrow", "Cooling off until 21:30". Tap it to open the limits.

Sensei knows your limits too. When you've lost a few in a row or keep asking it for calls, it can tell you to skip the next one and points you here ([Sensei](/docs/agents/sensei)).

Limits live in this browser, next to your record of entries.
