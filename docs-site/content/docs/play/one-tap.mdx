Your **stake per tap** starts at 5% of your balance on the ladder $0.05 · 0.10 · 0.25 · 0.50 · 1 · 2 · 5 · 10; tap it to type any amount within the round's bounds. Entries close 15 seconds before the round starts.

## Your call, live

Once you're in, the chart carries a **YOU ▲ UP** tag and tells you where you stand against <Line />, and a card says it in words:

| Moment | The card says |
|---|---|
| Nobody on the other side yet | Waiting for a Down call · refunded if nobody comes |
| Before the start | Starts 18:55 |
| Between the start and the line | Setting the line · signed within a minute |
| Running | Winning, Behind or Level, with how far |
| Done | Won +$0.24, Not this time, or Refunded |

## The chart

**Live** follows the round itself. **1H** shows an hour of 1-minute candles from the live line, and **1D** a day of 15-minute candles from signed prices (gold from the live line), as candles or a line. Small squares are each <Signed>signed price</Signed>.
