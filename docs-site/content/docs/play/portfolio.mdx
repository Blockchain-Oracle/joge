## Portfolio

Your tickets, read from the chain by your wallet's addresses. On top, **Net** with the story of your run: a film that walks your net up and down round by round, one beat and one sound each. Then **In · Back · Right**, entries still **On its way**, and every ticket with **▶ Replay** and **Receipt and video**. **Share my run** turns a period into a card or a video.

## Leaders

**24h · 7 days · All**. Players are ranked by net (paid back − paid in) over decided rounds, then by rounds played: a podium of three, then the field with "N rounds · N% right". Your own rows wear **you**. Every row opens the player.
