The first time you open Jōge, a five-step guide walks you through it. Each step moves on by itself when the thing happens: your wallet pairs, your coins land.

<Steps>
<Step>

### What it is

"Up or down, in minutes." One screen, one sentence. **Skip** is always there.

</Step>
<Step>

### Get a wallet

Any Bitcoin Cash wallet that speaks WizardConnect: <Logo name="cashonize.png" /> Cashonize, <Logo name="paytaca.png" /> Paytaca, <Logo name="selene.svg" /> Selene. Already have one? **I have one**.

</Step>
<Step>

### Pair it

Scan the code with your wallet, tap **Open wallet** on a phone, or **Open in Cashonize** on a desktop. The pairing message carries Jōge's name and icon, so your wallet shows who is asking. Pairing survives a refresh: you pair once.

</Step>
<Step>

### Get coins

On **chipnet**, tap **Get test BCH**: the faucet tops your wallet up to 2,000,000 test sats (about twenty calls) and shows the transaction on its way. On **mainnet**, the guide shows your address and a QR code to send a little BCH to.

</Step>
<Step>

### Call it

Tap **Up** or **Down**. Your wallet asks you to sign one transaction; that's the entry. A toast confirms "上 Up $0.10" and your call appears on the chart.

</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout title="Reopen the guide any time">
It opens once per device. Open it again from the **More** menu or with ⌘K → Guide.
</Callout>

## Where your money is

Your coins never pass through Jōge. The entry you sign moves your stake from your wallet straight into the round's gate contract, and the ticket it mints carries your payout address. When the round pays, the contract sends the money to that address. Your browser reads coins and broadcasts through public Fulcrum servers, not through our database.
