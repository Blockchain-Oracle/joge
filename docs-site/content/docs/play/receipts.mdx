---
title: Receipts and videos
description: "Turn any decided round you played into a receipt image or a video of the round, each with a QR code back to it."
---

**Receipt & video**, on any decided round you played and on every decided ticket in Portfolio, opens the share sheet.

<Facts>
  <Fact label="Receipt">A 1080×1350 card: the market and time, your call, what came back, the round's path against the line, the seal, and a QR code to the round.</Fact>
  <Fact label="Video">The round's film as an MP4, made in your browser: 1080×1350, 30 fps, with its own sound, ending on your result.</Fact>
  <Fact label="Hide amounts">Shows your return as a percentage instead of dollars.</Fact>
  <Fact label="Share">Your phone's share sheet with the file attached; on a desktop it saves the file and copies the link. Or Post on X.</Fact>
</Facts>

**Share my run** in Portfolio does the same for a stretch of time (today, the last 7 days, or all time) with your net, return, rounds and best round, as a card or a video, with a QR code to your profile.

> Browsers only allow video encoding on a secure page: open Jōge on its https address in Chrome or Safari. The receipt image works everywhere.
