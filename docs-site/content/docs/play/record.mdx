## Portfolio

Your tickets by your wallet's addresses, from Jōge's index of the chain. On top, your **net** with the story of your run: a film that walks it up and down, round by round. Then **In · Back · Right**, entries still on their way, and every ticket with ▶ Replay and Receipt and video.

## Leaders

**24h · 7 days · All**, ranked by net (paid back minus paid in) over decided rounds: a podium of three, then the rest of the top 50 with their rounds and hit rate.

## Profiles

Any Bitcoin Cash address: its marble, rank, story film, rounds, hit rate, best run, a 12-week grid, its agents and its tickets. No accounts: your address is your identity.
