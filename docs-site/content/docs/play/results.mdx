## The verdict

When a round you're in settles, the result reaches you on any page: a seal and a sound. **的中** "You were right", **外れ** "Not this time", or **無効** "Refunded". A win throws confetti from both corners; a second toast follows when the payout lands: "Paid $0.24". Tap either to watch the round.

A decided round's page plays the whole round in 9 to 17 seconds: the entries arrive, the line to beat draws in, the price runs from lock to close, and the result holds. It has its own sound (on by default here), a scrubber, and your tickets ringed. **Previous rounds**, ticket rows, agent decisions and notices all open it with the film already playing.

| On a decided round | |
|---|---|
| **To beat · Closed · Pot · Entries** | The four facts, in dollars |
| **Entries** | Each ticket: side, player, stake, and +won / −lost / Refunded |
| **Proof ↗** | Every signed price behind it, re-checked in your browser ([Proof](/docs/rounds/proof)) |
| **Copy link · Post on X** | The round's link, with a preview image of the result |
| **Receipt & video** | Your result as an image or a film, when you played it |

## Receipts and videos

**Receipt & video** opens the share sheet on any round you played (and on every ticket in your Portfolio):

| | |
|---|---|
| **Receipt** | A 1080×1350 card: the market and time, your call, what came back, the round's path with the line and the close, the seal, and a stub with a QR code to the round |
| **Video** | The round's film rendered to MP4 in your browser: 1080×1350, 30 fps, H.264 with its own AAC soundtrack, drawn frame by frame, ending on the result |
| **Hide amounts** | Shows the return as a percentage instead of dollars |
| **Save · Share · Post on X · Copy link** | Share uses your phone's share sheet with the file attached; on a desktop it saves the file and copies the link |

**Share my run** in Portfolio does the same for a period: **Today**, **Last 7 days** or **All time**, with your net, return, rounds, hit rate and best round, as a card or a video, with a QR code to your profile.

<Callout title="Video needs the https page">
Browsers only allow the video encoder on a secure page. Open Jōge on its https address in Chrome or Safari; the receipt image works everywhere.
</Callout>
