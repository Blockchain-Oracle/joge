When a round you're in settles, a seal and a sound reach you wherever you are: **的中** you were right, **外れ** not this time, **無効** refunded. A win throws confetti; a second note follows when the payout lands. Tap either to watch the round.

## The film

The whole round in 9 to 17 seconds: the entries arrive, <Line /> draws in, the price runs from start to close, and the result holds. It has a scrubber, its own sound, and your tickets ringed. **Previous rounds**, your tickets, agent decisions and notices all open it already playing.

| On a decided round | |
|---|---|
| To beat · Closed · Pot · Entries | The four facts |
| Entries | Each ticket: side, player, stake, result |
| Proof ↗ | Every signed price behind it ([3.3](/docs/price/proof)) |
| Receipt & video | Your result as an image or a film ([1.5](/docs/play/receipts)) |
