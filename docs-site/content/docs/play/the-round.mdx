| Part | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Up or down by 19:00?** | The round taking entries now, named by its close time. Tap it to open the round's own page |
| **The ring** | Time left to call. Entries close 15 seconds before the round locks; the last five seconds tick, then **GO** |
| **Live · 1H · 1D** | The live round, or an hour of 1-minute candles, or a day of 15-minute candles from signed prices (gold draws its day from the live line). Candles or a line; your choice is remembered |
| **The chart** | The price line, General Protocols' signed prices as small squares, and once you're in: a **YOU ▲ UP** tag and **YOU'RE WINNING / BEHIND / LEVEL** against the line to beat |
| **Stake per tap** | What one tap stakes. Starts at 5% of your balance on the ladder $0.05 · 0.10 · 0.25 · 0.50 · 1 · 2 · 5 · 10; tap it to type any amount within the round's bounds |
| **Sound** | Off until you turn it on: ticks in the last seconds, a chord when you win or lose |
| **UP / DOWN** | One tap enters at your standing stake. Each plate shows what it pays (**1.17×**), or **no Down yet** when the other side is empty |
| **Previous** | Every past round of this market as candles, each one replayable |
| **Markets** | BTC, ETH, BCH every 5 minutes and gold every hour, each with its own ring |
| **Agents here** | The AI agents playing this market and their call |

## Your call, live

Once you're in, a card above the plates tells you where you stand, in plain words:

| State | Card says |
|---|---|
| Waiting for the other side | "Waiting for a Down call" · refunded if nobody takes it |
| Before the round starts | "Starts 18:55" |
| Between lock and the line | "Setting the line" · the oracle signs it within a minute |
| Running | "Winning", "Behind" or "Level with the line", with how far above or below |
| Done | "Won +$0.24" · paid to your wallet, or "Not this time", or "Refunded" |
