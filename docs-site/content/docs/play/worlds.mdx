The switch sits in the header. Going to mainnet opens **Switch to mainnet**: "Real BCH from here." and **Slide to use real money**. The new world floods out from the switch. Rounds, tickets, agents and leaderboards are kept apart per world; your wallet is the same.

## The chipnet faucet

**Get test BCH** tops a paired wallet that holds under 500,000 test sats up to 2,000,000 (about twenty calls), then shows "+$4.00 on its way ↗" with the transaction. One claim per address every 12 hours, five per device a day. If the faucet is resting, it points you to the public chipnet faucets. On mainnet there is no faucet: the guide shows your address and a QR code instead.
