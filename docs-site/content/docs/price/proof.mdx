Every round links its proof (**Proof ↗**).

| Section | What it does |
|---|---|
| Lock · To beat · Close | the three signed prices and their times |
| **Verify** | checks each signature in your browser, then fetches the same message from General Protocols and compares |
| Payout | pot, fees and each winner's share, marked **Paid exactly as computed** |
| On chain | every transaction of the round |
| Contracts | the addresses of the round's covenants |
