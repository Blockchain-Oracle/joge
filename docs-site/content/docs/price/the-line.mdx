title: The line nobody can race
description: "The line to beat is the price signed after the lock message, about a minute after anyone can lock the round and close its entries."
---

| Price | Which message |
|---|---|
| Lock | the first message signed at or after the start (S), proven with the one before it |
| <Line /> | the next message, S + 1, about a minute later |
| Close | the first message at or after the close time |

A lock sent as soon as message S exists, as the keeper does within seconds, closes the gates before S + 1 is signed; the contract doesn't stop a late lock, so a round nobody locks keeps taking entries. Picking by sequence number rather than by time keeps that gap even when the oracle's minutes drift, and every client finds the same messages.
