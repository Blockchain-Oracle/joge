## Who sends each step

| Step | Sent by | Paid from |
|---|---|---|
| Open | Anyone (the keeper) | The opener's coins |
| Enter | Your wallet | Your coins |
| Lock | Anyone | The round's budget |
| Settle | Anyone | The round's budget |
| Pay | Anyone | The budget and each ticket's deposit |

## Lanes keep time

Each market and length is a **lane** with its own thread on chain. Its rounds start on a fixed grid (every five minutes for a 5-minute lane), and the thread opens only the next slot it hasn't opened, so two rounds for the same slot can't exist. While one round runs, the next takes entries. The chain has 13 lanes: 3, 5 and 15 minutes on BTC, ETH and BCH, and an hour on all four markets; the app plays four of them.
