Every round links its proof (**Proof ↗**). The page shows:

| Section | What you see |
|---|---|
| **Lock · To beat · Close** | The three signed prices with their times (gold shows both feeds) |
| **Verify N signatures** | Checks each message's Schnorr signature against the feed's public key, in your browser, then fetches the same message from General Protocols and compares. Each tile gets **Signature checked here** and **Same message at General Protocols** |
| **Payout** | pot − fees ÷ the winning side = pays ×; each winner with **Paid exactly as computed**, or "Everyone was refunded." |
| **On chain** | Every transaction of the round, oldest first |
| **Contracts · 5** | The covenant addresses: Gate, Ticket, Round · open, Round · locked, Round · paying |
